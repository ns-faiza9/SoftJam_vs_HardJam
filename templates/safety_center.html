🛡️ Safety Decision Center & Embedded Hardware Interlocks

Decoupled architecture: ML recommends operational actions; STM32 Cortex-M7 independently validates against hard physical limits.

STRICT CONTROL-PLANE ISOLATION POLICY

The ML engine on the laptop never writes directly to motor PWM registers, gate drivers, or PID gains. It can only recommend one of 4 discrete safe actions. The embedded STM32 firmware enforces local hardware safety interlocks and will autonomously reject any recommendation that violates thermal, overcurrent, or stall constraints.

1. ACCEPT_START

Normal inrush confirmed; healthy acceleration; transfer control to speed governor.

2. RETRY_SAFE_PROFILE

Marginal binding or low ML confidence. Retry with gentle pulsing declogging ramp.

3. DERATE

Soft jam with friction. Limit maximum drive torque by 30% to prevent thermal runaway.

4. STOP

Severe rotor lockup. Immediate electrical cutoff to protect stator windings.
